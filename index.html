About Euro Dragon

Customs clearance built for e-commerce volume

E-commerce shipments are different from traditional freight. One consignment can hold thousands of low-value parcels, each with its own sender, receiver and value. Euro Dragon is a customs agency that specialises in exactly that: declaring low-value e-commerce goods into the EU, parcel by parcel, at scale.

We work for e-commerce platforms, sellers and logistics partners shipping from outside the EU, mostly from Asia, into Europe. We file the declarations with Dutch Customs and make sure parcels are released and on their way to the customer.

01 · Air cargo

Cleared before the wheels touch down.

Most e-commerce volume into Europe arrives by air. We take the manifest as soon as the flight is booked, validate every parcel and lodge the H7 declarations before arrival. When the aircraft lands, the consignment is ready to be released.

FAQ

Questions shippers ask us

Something not covered here? Ask us directly.

What is e-commerce customs clearance?

Declaring goods bought online and shipped from outside the EU, usually in large consolidated shipments of individual parcels. Every parcel needs its own customs data, so the work is about handling high volumes quickly and accurately.

What is an H7 declaration?

H7 is the EU's reduced data set for customs declarations of low-value consignments: goods with an intrinsic value up to €150 sent to consumers in the EU. It was introduced when the €22 VAT exemption ended in July 2021.

What changed on 1 July 2026?

The €150 customs duty relief for low-value consignments ended. An interim flat-rate customs duty of €3 now applies to low-value e-commerce goods. These consignments are still declared with H7, so accurate parcel data matters more than ever.

IOSS or non-IOSS: what is the difference?

With IOSS, the seller charges EU VAT at checkout and pays it through the Import One-Stop Shop, so the parcel is released without VAT at import. Without IOSS, import VAT is paid at the border and collected from the receiver. We handle both.

What data do you need from us?

A manifest with parcel-level data: sender and receiver, goods description, quantity, value, weight and, where available, the HS code. We validate the data and flag problems before anything is filed.

Which modes of transport do you cover?

Air, road and sea. Most e-commerce volume arrives by air; sea freight suits steady, less urgent volume, and road links hubs and parcel networks across the EU.
